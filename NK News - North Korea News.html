DOJ subpoenas journalist over North Korea SEAL mission report

Reporter slams subpoena as push to ‘force disclosure’ of confidential sources behind claims of 2019 infiltration attempt
Shreyas Reddy August 3, 2026
SHARE
COPY LINK FACEBOOK X LINKEDIN MASTODON
Navy SEALs conducting dive operations training in the Atlantic Ocean | Image: U.S. Navy photo (May 29, 2019)

The U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) has reportedly subpoenaed an investigative journalist over a New York Times report about an alleged secret operation to infiltrate North Korea during President Donald Trump’s first term, which the reporter framed as an effort to force him to reveal confidential sources.

In a statement posted on social media on Saturday, freelance journalist Matthew Cole said he received the subpoena in connection with a Sept. 2025 investigation he co-authored with The Times’ correspondent Dave Philipps, which detailed a previously undisclosed Navy SEAL Team 6 mission in 2019.

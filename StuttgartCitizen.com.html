Duty Bus weekend and (U.S.) holiday routes are suspended

On: August 3, 2026
In: Announcements, Kelley Barracks, News, Panzer Kaserne, Patch Barracks, Robinson Barracks, Service Adjustments

By USAG Stuttgart Public Affairs

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by

Instaread

Due to personnel shortages the weekend and U.S.-holiday duty bus routes have been suspended until further notice. This change will take effect August 8 and remain in place until further notice.

Take a look at the new duty bus schedule here and be sure to read the duty bus FAQs. For the most up to date information and schedules, be sure to download the My Army Post App.

The Most Popular Birthday Party Activities by Age Group

Your 13-year-old would probably rather eat dirt than sit through a repeat of their little sibling’s Minion-themed party. The right activities can turn any event into the party of the year, but the wrong ones? Cue the dramatic teenage eye-rolls and guests plotting their early escape.

Here’s a breakdown of the best birthday party activities at Stars and Strikes by age group, so every kid experiences a fun celebration.

Birthday Party Activities for Toddlers (Ages 2–4)

Toddlers have big energy, short attention spans, and zero interest in following rules. Keep things simple and low-pressure.

Simple Games and Free Play

At this age, unstructured play is actually the goal. Think bubbles, soft play areas, or simple games like duck-duck-goose. Toddlers don’t need complex rules; they need space to run and things to grab.

Bowling with Ramps and Bumpers

Toddler bowling is the cutest thing ever. Bumper lanes prevent gutter balls, and ramps help their tiny arms launch that ball like a pro. Every knock-down gets a cheer, and nobody goes home crying about their score.

Arcade Games Designed for Younger Children

Many arcades offer games built specifically for little ones—simple button-pressing, coin-dropping, and ticket-winning fun. These games are all rewarding with very little frustration.

PJ 10 AM Birthday Parties: A Great Morning Option for Early Risers

Stars and Strikes offers PJ parties starting at 10 AM—perfect for toddlers who are at peak happiness before noon. Guests can choose bowling or arcade fun, all before anyone hits their cranky time.

Birthday Party Activities for Young Kids (Ages 5–7)

Kids in this age group are ready for a little friendly competition. They understand rules, love winning, and are just coordinated enough to actually play.

Bowling Games and Friendly Competitions

Many kids this age start wanting a real challenge. Keeping score adds excitement without taking the fun too seriously.

Arcade Challenges

Arcade games become more engaging at this age. Kids can aim for high scores, chase tickets, and focus on specific games they love. Expect to hear this phrase often: “just one more game!”

Prize Redemption Fun

Winning tickets and trading them in for prizes? That’s basically the highlight of the entire party. It doesn’t matter if the prize is a bouncy ball; the process of earning and choosing is addictive.

Group Activities That Keep Everyone Engaged

The key at this age is keeping all guests involved. Rotating between bowling and arcade stations means nobody stands around waiting, and everyone stays entertained from start to finish.

Birthday Party Activities for Older Kids (Ages 8–12)

This group is ready to compete—seriously. They want bragging rights, high scores, and a real challenge.

Team Bowling Competitions

Split the group into teams and make it a tournament. Older kids thrive with structure and stakes, and team bowling turns a lane into a full-on sporting event.

Arcade Tournaments

Head-to-head arcade competitions keep this age group locked in. Whether it’s racing games, basketball shootouts, or ticket-earning machines, the drive to win keeps energy levels high.

Interactive Attractions

Laser tag, escape rooms, and other interactive attractions are perfect for this group. These activities reward teamwork, strategy, and a little bit of sneakiness—all things older kids excel at.

Why All Access Parties Are Popular for This Age

Stars and Strikes All Access party packages bundle multiple attractions together, so kids can bounce between bowling, arcade games, and other activities. For a group with a wide range of interests, it’s the ultimate solution, since nobody gets stuck doing something they’re bored with.

Birthday Party Activities for Teens (Ages 13+)

Teens are a different beast entirely. They want autonomy, competition, and the freedom to hang out on their own terms.

Competitive Bowling

Teens take bowling seriously. Real scoring, no bumpers, and a leaderboard to argue about afterward.

Multiplayer Arcade Games

Side-by-side arcade games spark natural competition between friends. Teens love the challenge of beating each other’s scores, and a good arcade gives them plenty of opportunities to do exactly that.

Group Challenges and Leaderboard Competitions

Laser tag is practically made for teenagers. Strategy, teamwork, and the thrill of eliminating your friends? That’s a birthday memory right there.

Flexible Entertainment That Lets Guests Choose Their Favorite Activities

Unlike younger kids, teens don’t need a tightly scheduled itinerary. Giving them access to multiple activities and letting them choose keeps everyone happy and the vibe relaxed.

How to Choose the Right Birthday Party Activities

Start with the birthday child’s age and interests. Then think about the guests attending—different ability levels, energy types, and group sizes all affect what works.

Mixing active activities (like bowling or laser tag) with slightly more relaxed ones (like arcade games) gives everyone a chance to recharge. Packages that bundle multiple entertainment options are especially useful for larger groups with varied interests.

Why Bowling and Arcade Parties Appeal to Every Age

Bowling and arcade parties keep showing up on birthday wish lists for a reason. They work across the board—for tiny toddlers and competitive teens alike.

  • Easy for all skill levels, with options like bumper lanes for younger kids
  • Encourages friendly competition without taking itself too seriously
  • Indoor and weather-proof, so the weather doesn’t ruin the day
  • Something genuinely fun for every single guest

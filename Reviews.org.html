The top 10 best TV channels

1. HBO: Best for prestige TV lovers

If you're looking for highly acclaimed shows with complex storytelling, HBO is the top destination. Home to hits like "Game of Thrones" and "The Sopranos," it's a premium channel — and one you'll have to pay up for. But that's rarely an issue for fans, since it's hard to find HBO content that isn't already generating buzz. Personally, I love it for how consistently rich its shows feel.

Popular current shows:

  • “House of the Dragon”
  • “Rooster”
  • “Hacks”

2. NBC: Best for broadcast generalists

NBC covers a lot of ground, from gripping dramas and popular reality TV to Sunday Night Football. Comedy fans have long been drawn to the channel, dating back to the days of “Seinfeld” and “The Office.” That tradition continues with popular hits like “St. Denis Medical.” If you’re unable to get the channel via an over-the-air antenna, you can access it on most platforms.

Popular current shows:

  • “Law & Order: Special Victims Unit”
  • “Happy’s Place”
  • “Chicago P.D.”

3. ESPN: Best for sports fans

ESPN is a must-have for most sports fans. The network airs games from all four major sports leagues, so it’s the destination for fans who want access to a wide range of events. If there’s an important game, it’s more likely than not that it’s on ESPN. And it has been that way for years.

Best current shows:

  • “The Pat McAfee Show”
  • “Monday Night Football”
  • “First Take”

4. CBS: Best for procedural drama fans

Most fans of CBS go to the channel for one thing: the gritty procedural drama. My family, like many others, loved the CSI franchise. The network continues that tradition today with other frequently watched drama hits. The network isn’t just for drama fans, either. Popular reality series like “The Amazing Race” and “Survivor,” not to mention Sunday afternoon NFL games, make it a must-have for many.

Popular current shows:

  • “Tracker”
  • “NCIS”
  • “Ghosts”

5. FX: Best for critically acclaimed originals

FX is often an overlooked network. For years, it has pushed the envelope with its original content. Shows like “The Americans” or “Sons of Anarchy” aired years ago, but they still hold their value today. FX’s content isn’t quite HBO-level prestige, but it's never been far off.

Popular current shows:

  • “The Bear”
  • “It’s Always Sunny in Philadelphia”
  • “American Horror Story”

6. PBS: Best for documentary buffs

PBS is a rich resource for documentary lovers. The network is the home of Ken Burns’ acclaimed documentaries as well as top-notch investigative journalism. PBS isn’t just a destination for documentary buffs, either. It has long been a top choice for kids’ content like “Sesame Street” and “Daniel Tiger’s Neighborhood.”

Popular current shows:

  • “Masterpiece”
  • “Wild Kratts”
  • “Frontline”

7. Disney Channel: Best for families

Disney is practically synonymous with family content. The Disney Channel is essential for nearly any family with young children, but it's more than just “Mickey Mouse Clubhouse.” It also has popular content for tweens who are no longer merely interested in animated content. If you don’t want a subscription to Disney+, the Disney Channel provides loads of popular content.

Popular current shows:

  • “Big City Greens”
  • “Phineas and Ferb”
  • “Miraculous: Tales of Ladybug & Cat Noir”

8. Bravo: Best for reality TV fans

If you're a reality TV fan, you know how important it is to have Bravo. Many of the network's reality shows go viral as fans dissect every twist. Franchises like "The Real Housewives" may flaunt extravagant lifestyles, but fans still relate to the real struggles the cast members face.

Popular current shows:

  • "Vanderpump Rules"
  • "The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City"
  • "Southern Charm"

9. FS1: Best for live sports and racing

Sports fans know that ESPN isn't the only game in town. FS1 has been another key sports network for years, especially for racing fans and college football fans like me. It's long been the place to catch Cup Series races and Craftsman Truck Series action.

Popular current content:

  • NASCAR races
  • “First Things First”
  • Saturday afternoon MLB games

10. Discovery Channel: Best for science programming

The Discovery Channel isn't just stuffy and educational. For years, it has employed a character-driven approach that injects reality-show energy into its science programming. Yes, you can learn something watching it, but it's the real-life human stories that keep viewers coming back.

Best current shows:

  • “Deadliest Catch”
  • “Gold Rush”
  • “In the Eye of the Storm”

How we came up with our rating

How we came up with our rating

We evaluated dozens of basic and premium cable networks based on three primary pillars:

Current programming quality: A great network needs a reason to tune in today. We prioritized channels that consistently deliver critically acclaimed original series, high-quality unscripted television, and live events.

Cultural impact: We rewarded networks that have actively shaped the television landscape.

Availability and value: We factored in how easily viewers can access these networks across major TV providers and live TV streaming services to ensure you are getting the best bang for your buck in your TV package.

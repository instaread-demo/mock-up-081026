Niagara’s oldest sporting franchise begins Ontario Finals Friday at home

by Rod Mawhood

July 31, 2026  |   2 min read

For a season that many predicted would be a rebuild, or at least retool, the St. Catharines Jr. A Athletics have surprised many.

Over 20 players graduated from the 2025 Athletics who of course hosted the Minto Cup.

This year the A’s have a chance to get back to the Canadian Junior A Lacrosse Championship after finishing the regular season in first place with a (15-3) record and playoff series wins over both Burlington and Orangeville.

“We’re proud of finishing at the top of the table in the regular season, and the work we’ve put in so far in the first two rounds of the playoffs,” explained head coach Dan Coates.

“We know we have a long way to go to reach our ultimate goal but we will continue to take it one game at a time.”

Coates, a first year bench boss, was joined by Associate Head Coach Randy Mearns and Assistants Ian Llord and Corey Small.

The Whitby Warriors (9-1 in the postseason) will provide the opposition for the A’s in the Ontario Finals.

Max Wetherup leads the Warriors in playoff goals (22), assists (43) and points (65).

Jameson Bucktooth tops St. Catharines with 48 postseason points.

One intriguing matchup sees the battle of brothers in goal as Ty Wilson will get the nod in net for the Athletics, younger brother Cooper Wilson for Whitby.

“Our boys have put in a lot of work this summer and we want to continue this journey in the finals. We want to compete against the best so we’re looking forward to this opportunity,” Coates said.

The best of seven series begins Friday night at Canada Games Park in Thorold.

“We love seeing our community getting behind this team. We’re hoping to inspire the next generation of lacrosse players in St. Catharines and Niagara,” added Coates.

The Victoria Shamrocks and Coquitlam Adanacs in British Columbia, and the Calgary Jr. A Mounties and Raiders Jr. A Lacrosse clubs are the finalists in Alberta.

The Minto Cup begins August 16 in Calgary with the Mounties as hosts.

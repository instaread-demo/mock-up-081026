Neighbor used to be a pro and instructor, so the family interest is high.

What’s in the Box & Key Features

This set includes:

5-section lightweight fiberglass (glass fiber) poles that assemble into a full 6-foot flagstick
Strong connectors designed to stay together in the wind
A high-quality ABS putting cup with six drainage holes so water doesn’t pool
A bright red 420D nylon flag (approx. 19″ × 13″) with an O-ring/snap system that lets the flag spin freely instead of wrapping up and binding

The poles go together in a staggered red-and-white pattern (or all one color if you prefer). There’s a loop at the top and a simple system so the flag can rotate freely. The whole thing breaks down small enough to toss in a golf bag or store easily.


Our Install Experience (Watch the Video)


In the video we show the whole process on our place.


We used an old 4-inch hole saw (don’t ruin a good one—this will dull the teeth, especially in rocky or rooty ground) to cut a hole about 4 inches deep. The ground was dry, so it was a little dusty, but it worked fine. We leveled the cup with a rubber mallet so there’s no lip for the ball to catch.


Safety first: Always call 811 before you dig or drill anywhere. We already knew where our power and sewer lines were, but you never know.


Assembly is dead simple—just stack the sections and attach the flag. The rotating O-ring design is a nice touch so the flag doesn’t get tangled in the wind. We put a couple of holes in opposite corners of the field for good practice distance without losing too many balls. Then we mowed a rough fairway between them.


It took almost no time and now we have a little short course right on the homestead. Perfect for family practice, chipping, and putting.

What Amazon Customers Are Saying
The product currently sits around 4.2 out of 5 stars based on well over 1,000 ratings. Here’s the honest summary of real buyer feedback:
What people love:

Extremely easy and quick to put together and install
Excellent value for creating backyard or driving-range style practice holes
Poles and cup feel solid for the price
Bright, highly visible flag
Great for family fun, kids learning the game, or just practicing approach shots and short game at home
Drainage holes in the cup work as advertised

Common complaints:

The strings/ties that hold the flag to the pole are the weak point. Many reviewers report them breaking, rotting, or fraying after a few weeks to a couple of months of continuous outdoor exposure
Some flags start to fray or tear with prolonged wind and weather
A few note that plastic connectors can become brittle over time if left out year-round

The consensus is clear: the poles and cup hold up well, but treat the flag as a consumable. Bring the flag (or the whole stick) inside when you’re not using it, or replace the string with something stronger (paracord works great). Do that and most owners are very happy with the set.


Final Thoughts
For the money, this is an easy win if you want to practice at home without spending a fortune on a full artificial green. It’s not tournament-grade equipment, but it doesn’t need to be. We now have functional practice holes on the property, the family is more motivated to hit balls, and setup was straightforward.


If you’re looking to add a couple of backyard targets or a mini short-course feel to your yard, garden, or field, this THIODOON 6ft flagstick and cup set gets the job done.

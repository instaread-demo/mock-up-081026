Memorandum in Opposition to the proposed Valley-North Transmission Line

Posted on August 3, 2026August 3, 2026 Author Talk2shariComment(1) 591 Views
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 2:06
Instaread

The Little Kanawha Area Development Corporation submits this letter in its opposition to the proposed Valley-

North Transmission Line. A review of the proposal and consideration of concerns raised by businesses,

organizations and residents, leads to the conclusion that this project brings no meaningful benefits to Calhoun

County and its surrounding communities throughout West Virginia. The negative impact and dire consequences

such as environmental disruption, economic strain, and lasting visual impacts would bring harm to any and all

areas impacted, while most, if not all, benefits would flow to interests located outside our county and state.

Little Kanawha Area Development Corporation’s mission is to promote economic growth that is responsible

and appropriate for our region. We continue to invest in expanding recreation and business opportunities,

including ongoing tourism development tied to the Little Kanawha River Trail, The Dark Skies Project, our

county parks, farming and small business investments. This proposed corridor threatens these longstanding

efforts while providing no direct payoff for those who live and work here.

LKADC is also concerned about potential health-related effects. Many community members have voiced

apprehension about long-term risks associated with adding new transmission infrastructure near homes,

businesses, schools and local communities. Even where some issues are still being studied, we believe these

concerns warrant careful attention, a transparent evaluation, and appropriate responses as part of a rigorous

review. The well-being of our residents must remain a central factor in any decision.

Our residents and businesses play a real role in the process, along with protection of their property rights and

safeguards for their communities. Decisions should reflect the interests of West Virginians first, rather than

outside priorities.

Little Kanawha Area Development Corporation stands with our local leaders, businesses and community

members in opposition to the Valley-North Transmission Line and respectfully requests that the permitting

agencies either deny the request or require alternative approaches that do not impose an unnecessary burden on

Calhoun County, its residents and businesses.

Thank you for your time and consideration, and for ensuring this correspondence is included in the official

record.

Respectfully,

Shelia Burch, Executive Director

Weekend Roundup: July 24-26

From the Cincinnati Music Festival to Art After Hours summer reading finale, there is so much to celebrate this weekend.  

Cincinnati Music Festival  

Date: July 23-25 
Time: 7 p.m.  
Location: Paycor Stadium, Cincinnati  

The Cincinnati Music Festival will take place July 23-25 and will feature artists such as Charlie Wilson, Mary J Bilge, Heatwave, 803Fresh and Trey Songz. Friday’s concert will feature special guest Nelly. Originally a jazz concert in 1962, the Cincinnati Music Festival invites attendees to enjoy R&B, jazz, soul and hip hop artists for a weekend to remember.  

Art After Hours  

Date: July 24  
Time: 7-9 p.m.  
Location: Carrico/Fort Thomas Branch, Campbell County Public Library, Fort Thomas, KY  

The Carrico/Fort Thomas Branch invites community members to the Campbell County Library Adult Summer Reading Finale celebration. New and returning artists feature their artwork and will sell them at the event. Music is provided by the Cincinnati Contemporary Jazz Orchestra, hors d’oeuvres by Kate’s Catering and flower arrangements by Fort Thomas Florist and Greenhouses. Guests can also create their own tiny artwork in the Tiny Art Show and vote on the winning piece.  

Cincinnati Black Music Walk of Fame Induction Ceremony & Dedication Celebration 

Date: July 25 
Time: Noon.  
Location: 190 W. Mehring Way, Cincinnati  

The Cincinnati Black Music Walk of Fame welcomes community members to celebrate the 2026 inductees and their musical legacy through the Induction Ceremony and Dedication Celebration on July 25. Guests can also enjoy live indoor and outdoor concerts and are invited to wear all white to celebrate.  

Queen City 5K 

Date: July 25 
Time: 8 a.m. 
Location: Downtown Cincinnati 

Run, walk and help fundraise scholarship funds for local students through the Queen City 5k, organized by Queen City Foundation’s Alumni and Parent Association. According to the foundation’s website, the course starts near Paycor Stadium, continues down the river, continues along Smale Park, passes Great American Ball Park and Heritage Bank Arena. The race will also include age group awards for first place runners in each division. Entertainment will also be provided during and after the race.  

Butler County Ohio Fair Opening Day 

Date: July 26  
Time 7 a.m.-11 p.m.  
Location: 1715 Fairgrove Ave, Hamilton 

The Butler County Ohio Fair invites visitors to the opening day of the fair which runs through August 1. The fair includes rides, food and drink, live music, auctions, livestock shows and special events for everyone to enjoy.
